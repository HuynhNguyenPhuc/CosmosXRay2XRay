# WORKFLOW.md — Research & Strategy Guidelines

- Select the optimal workflow path to reach target execution goals efficiently.

- Continuously refine agent skills and research tracking workflows.
- Review key design decisions and training logs daily.
- Target top-tier conferences (MICCAI, NeurIPS, CVPR, IEEE TMI).
